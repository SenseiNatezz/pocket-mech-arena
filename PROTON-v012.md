# Proton-style blast — v0.12.0

Inspired by the supplied Iron Man Proton Cannon clip: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/WomQuf471DM

The Gundam's raised weapon now fires a broad turquoise beam with a white core, streaming energy streaks, expanding muzzle rings, and pink-white impact flashes. This adapts the blast effect to the existing rifle rather than copying the separate oversized cannon model.

Charge: 0.62 seconds. Sustained beam: 1.15 seconds. Recovery returns to the raised-rifle flight pose. The beam follows the animated muzzle and keeps its selected firing direction. Each enemy in the wider beam takes damage once per activation, including enemies that enter during the blast. Cooldown remains 12 seconds; damage remains eight times the rifle damage. Movement speed remains 6.5 units/second.
