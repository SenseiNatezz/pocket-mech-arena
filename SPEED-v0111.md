# Faster Gundam movement — v0.11.1

Base movement speed increases from 5.2 to 6.5 units/second (+25%). Equipment and Faster Thrusters upgrades multiply the new base speed. The rifle remains raised throughout movement.

Ordinary enemy pursuit speeds range from 1.2 to 2.6 units/second, with bosses at 0.8. Rush drones and the Assault Striker briefly charge at 8 units/second after a warning. The joystick scales movement with how far it is pushed, so a partial push produces a lower speed.
